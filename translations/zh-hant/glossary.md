# zh-hant glossary (Traditional Chinese, Taiwan usage, 國小五年級)

Adapted from the reviewed Simplified version (`zh/`), checked against the English. One chosen term per English term, used everywhere.

Style:
- Full-width punctuation in Chinese sentences: ，。：；！？（）、 and Taiwan quotation marks 「」 (inner 『』), never “ ” or ‘ ’. Book titles 《》. Ellipsis ……
- A space between Chinese and Latin letters or digits ("160 mm", "第 9 課", "Tinkercad 裡"), as in the Simplified version. Units, numbers, rule numbers and symbols unchanged.
- Taiwan school and computer vocabulary: 列印, 檔案, 螢幕, 搜尋, 連結, 預設, 裝置, 儲存, 載入, 選取, 拖曳, 置中, 匯出, 複製, 塑膠, 引擎, 公斤, 義大利, 漸層, 相簿, 繳交, 即時, 範例, 夥伴.
- Avoid 幹 (幹什麼) in children's text; use 做什麼.
