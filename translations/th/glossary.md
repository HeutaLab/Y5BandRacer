# Thai glossary (th) — The Constructor's Brief

One chosen term per concept. Used the same way in every entry.

## Style decisions

- Reader is addressed as **คุณ** where a pronoun is needed; often the pronoun is dropped, as Thai allows.
- The teachers / school ("we", "our") are **ครู** when they act ("ครูจะเปิดไฟล์ของคุณ"), **ของเรา** for the school's printers.
- No ครับ/ค่ะ. Friendly particles (นะ, เลย, กัน) used sparingly.
- No full stops at the end of Thai sentences; a space separates sentences. `?` kept on questions (normal in Thai school worksheets).
- Spaces around Latin words and digits: `รถ Formula 1 ทุกคัน`, `ยาว 160 mm`.
- Where the English writes a number as a word ("two or three", "four times"), the Thai also uses a word, so the digits match.
- Rule references: a specific rule in prose is **กฎข้อ 2.7**; the short labels in the scrutineering card keep **R 2.3**. "The rules" in general = **กติกา**.
