# Korean (ko) glossary — The Constructor's Brief

Register: 해요체 for instructions and explanations (써요, 골라요, 해요). Headings may be short noun phrases.
Tinkercad buttons stay in English (Box, Cylinder, Text, Solid, Hole, Group, Align, Mirror, Drop, Duplicate, Edit Grid, Export, "Everything in the design", Sides, Front, Right, dashboard). A Korean gloss in brackets is added once or twice only: Align(정렬), Group(그룹으로 합치기), Sides 64(옆면 개수), Edit Grid(격자 편집), Duplicate(복제), Mirror → 좌우 반전.
