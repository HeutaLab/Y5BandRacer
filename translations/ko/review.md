## Changes

### Meaning and Tinkercad instructions

- k0384: "차축 높이 − 5**만큼** 올려요" means "raise it *by* axle height − 5". In Tinkercad, a cylinder turned on its side is usually already off the workplane, so a child raising it *by* that amount ends up too high. → "…인 곳까지 올려요" (raise it *to* that height). This matches the k0597 label "부싱을 올릴 높이".
- k0385: the same "만큼" problem for the axle hole → "차축 높이 − 3인 곳까지 올려요".
- k0369: "상자를 차 위로 밀어서" reads as "push the box onto the top of the car" → "상자를 옮겨 차를 감싸게 하고, 상자 바닥이 작업 평면에 닿게 한 다음…" (slide it over the car).
- k0540: "결승 측정에서 한 밀리미터를 내주게 돼요" did not clearly say that distance is lost → "차가 한 밀리미터 길어질 때마다, R 9.2에 따라 마지막에 재는 거리가 한 밀리미터씩 줄어요." k0254 already said "잃어요" clearly; no change was needed there.
- k0046: "무게 제한보다 한 킬로그램도 안 되게 가벼워서" could be read as "the car weighed under 1 kg" → "최소 무게에서 한 킬로그램도 안 되게 모자라서".
- k0019: "160 mm 이하인 차에 들어갈 수 있어야" said the name must fit *inside* the car → "…작은 차에 써넣을 수 있어야" (it must fit *on* the car).
- k0047: "크기 검사 상자를 통과해야" → "크기 검사 상자에 쏙 들어가야". This matches k0196 and the meaning "fit inside the box".
- k0059: the question had lost "how long your car *should* be" → "이 규칙을 보면, 차를 얼마나 길게 만들어야 할지 무엇을 알 수 있을까요?"
- k0308: "가장 많이 방해할 힘" plus a list that was left hanging (several items are not forces) → "더 멀리 가지 못하게 실제로 막는 것은 무엇이라고 생각하나요? … 아니면 공기 저항일까요?"
- k0298: "단단히 고정해야" did not carry "mechanically retained" → "신고해야 하고, 빠지지 않게 단단히 고정해야 해요."
- k0330: "중심선 위" is ambiguous in a table about heights ("above the centre line") → "중심선 자리".
- k0489: the same ambiguity → "앞쪽 아래 중심선에 있음".
- k0449: "바퀴가 옆으로 세워져" is ambiguous; the wheel is standing on its edge → "세로로 세워져".
- k0452: "옆으로 서 있음" → "세로로 서 있음", for the same reason.
- k0200: the column header "정해진 프레임의 값" was vague → "프레임이 정해 주는 값" (what the set frame gives you).

### Naturalness, register and the 10-year-old reader

- k0123: "평가하지 않아요" sounds adult and official, and "차가 무엇인지 설계하기 전에" was stiff and literal → "점수에 들어가지 않아요" and "차 <em>자체</em>를 설계하기 전에".
- k0126: "자동차 경주 역사의 대부분 동안" is translationese → "자동차 경주에서는 오랫동안".

- k0141: "화면을 가득 채운 다음" made the child the one who fills the screen → "화면에 꽉 차게 한 다음".
- k0181: the same fix → "사진을 눌러서 화면에 꽉 차게 해요".
- k0143: the link title "노즈와 앞날개 가까이" was unnatural → "노즈와 앞날개를 가까이서".
- k0147: "모든 검색에 덧붙이기" → "검색어에 덧붙이기". The buttons add words to the search terms.

- k0255: "날개 너비 (양옆)" was awkward → "날개 너비 (좌우 길이)". It now parallels k0256 "날개 앞뒤 길이".
- k0615: "Size" → "인원" is correct, but "팀원 수" is plainer and matches k0014 "팀원은 몇 명인가요?" (the cell shows just "2" or "3").

## Glossary

No term changes. The glossary choices are sound and are used consistently: 차량 검사, 검사관, 크기 검사 상자, 설계 기록지, 자료 조사실, 차시/반/부문, 가지런히 놓기 (knolling), 출력판, 작업 평면, 끼우는 바퀴 / 고정 바퀴.

The only gap was the per-tab gloss rule for 지상고 and 서포트, fixed above. One note: "단원" should not be used for page sections (k0004).

## Overall verdict

This is a good translation. It is accurate, and almost entirely in friendly 해요체 with short sentences. The F1 tone comes through well: "옆에 앉아 구경만 하는 사람은 없어요" and "구경하는 게 아니라 조사하는 거예요" both read well.

Tags, placeholders, numbers and Tinkercad names were all handled correctly. The real problems were few:

- two Tinkercad instructions ("만큼 올려요") that could make a child raise the bushing and hole to the wrong height
- "slide over the car" read as "onto the top of the car"
- k0540 not saying plainly that distance is lost
- a handful of literal or adult-sounding phrases

## For a native-speaking teacher to look at

- **On a real iPad set to Korean,** check the wording of Safari's "Add to Photos" ("사진에 추가" or "'사진' 앱에 저장") and Procreate's 동작 / 추가 / 사진 삽입하기. Also check the Bing "Images" tab (이미지). If the class iPads are in Korean, the English menu words could be dropped and only the Korean kept.
- **Words that may be new to 10-year-olds,** each defined or glossed once: 컨스트럭터, 리버리, 지상고, 부싱, 캘리퍼스, 다운포스, 슬라이서 프로그램. Decide whether these work for your class or need saying aloud.
- **Class labels:** check that 감기형 / 고리형 match how the teacher will actually describe the two classes in Korean, and that "부문" is not too formal for the children.
- **k0298:** check whether "빠지지 않게 단단히 고정" is the right reading of "mechanically retained" in Rule 3.3. For example, does tape count?
- **k0046:** "심판들" is used for race officials. This is fine for children, but "담당자들" is the more exact word if preferred.
